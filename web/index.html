What the observations mean

Seen, not guaranteed. AIS receiver coverage varies. A quiet map may mean no reception, not an empty harbour. Destinations are vessel-reported text, not confirmed itineraries.

Time observed at anchor. Two minutes of compatible reports inside an approximate anchorage zone are required. A long observation gap resets the timer. This is not an official waiting-time statistic.

Your privacy. Search and filters run locally. An optional collector address is saved only if requested. The collector receives your requests; map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap. No AIS key belongs in this page.
